# Design System: IWAC Theme

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Research Broadsheet"**

A 20th-century broadsheet operated as a scientific instrument. The site is a scholarly
database — LLM sentiment models, LDA topics, embeddings, IIIF — that carries the visual
grammar of the newspapers it preserves: masthead wordmark, uppercase section strip,
thick 2px ink rules, datelines, ledger-style result rows, a press collage printed on a
blue plate. The execution is a modern broadsheet's digital archive run by a university
press: newspaper typographic conventions with Linear/Stripe-grade product polish.

Ink is the brand; blue is the spot colour. A broadsheet prints in black and adds one
second plate where it matters, and since 2.33 the system does exactly that: square
corners, no elevation on the page, no cards — sections divided by rules on one flat
`--surface` — and a deep press blue used the way a second plate is used. It replaced a
burnt orange on rounded, shadowed cards with tracked uppercase labels everywhere, which
together read as a generic template product rather than an archive.

Density is a feature, not a compromise: researchers want more items per screen, flat
ledger rows over boxed cards, metadata flowing inline with interpunct separators, short
prose flowing into newspaper columns rather than sitting in a capped measure. Restraint
is the resting state — the quiet control is the default and loudness opts in. Confirmed
anti-references: not a small museum's website, not a warm editorial magazine, not a
generic dashboard, not a SaaS card grid; no cream or parchment, no pastiche of aged
paper.

The system is enforced, not merely written down. Every value the token generator
publishes is compared by a guard in this repo and in both consumer modules; a rule that
lives only in prose is a rule that has already drifted. That doctrine — publish it and
assert it — is why the palette below includes the twenty categorical chart slots and the
eleven resource-type and entity hues.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Ink and one spot colour: a deep press blue, used where a second plate would be
- Square and flat: zero radii, no shadows on the page, rules instead of boxes
- Newspaper grammar (rules, datelines, ledgers, columns) executed with product-grade polish
- Uppercase rationed to the nav strip and datelines; every other label is sentence case
- Near-white light mode; warm "lamplit reading room" dark mode, declared in full as a mixin pair
- AI-generated content visibly marked (computational honesty)

## Colors

An OKLCH-derived palette: near-white warm-tinted surfaces, cool-neutral inks, one press-blue
spot colour, a claret data colour, and two encoding scales (resource type, chart series),
all cascading from two admin-overridable seeds via `color-mix(in oklab, …)`.

### Primary
- **Press Blue** (seed `--primary-base` #1c2f9e; working `--primary` is the seed darkened
  8%): the spot colour. Appears as links, focus state, current/active state (nav tab,
  active filter chips, pressed toggles), dateline interpuncts, categorical dots, key
  counts, the one filled button, and the plate — the homepage hero ground and the plate
  page head. Never heading text, per-row badge fills, decorative wash or gradient. Hover
  and pressed deepen to the seed darkened 18% / 28%. As text it measures 10.7–11.8:1 on
  every light surface, so it is legible as link text anywhere. Dark mode does not tint the
  seed toward white — that bleaches a deep blue grey — but **re-lights** it: lightness
  pulled to ~78% with the seed's own hue and chroma (#7aa2ff, 7.3–8.2:1 on the lamplit
  surfaces).

### Secondary
- **Claret** (seed `--secondary-base`): **data-only** — chart series slot 2 and corpus
  comparison in IwacVisualizations, plus the `publication` resource-type dot. Dark mode
  mixes it 30% toward white (#ae6770). Never buttons, links, focus, or any UI chrome. It
  replaced a slate that sat beside navy and a blue `--info` as three blues in one bar.

### Neutral
- **Ink Strong**: masthead rules, headings, section-opening rules, strongest emphasis.
- **Ink**: body text and metadata labels.
- **Ink Light / Ink Subtle / Muted**: secondary text, datelines, quiet chrome, captions;
  `--muted` clears AA (≥4.5:1) on every surface including sunken.
- **Ink On Pastel**: the one text ink used on the tinted status grounds.
- **Ink On Primary**: text and icons painted *on* a `--primary` fill — filled buttons and
  submits, the add/remove-value buttons' hover, a checked box's tick. White in light
  (12.1:1 at rest); warm dark ink (#0f0a05) in dark mode, where white on the re-lit blue
  falls to 2.49:1 and the dark ink reads 7.92 / 9.99 / 6.29:1 at rest / hover / pressed.
  Never a hardcoded `--white`.
- **Surface / Raised / Sunken / Background**: near-white at chroma ~0.002 — an
  imperceptible warm tint, never cream. The body paints `--surface`: the page is one
  flat sheet. `--background` stays published for consumers but the page no longer paints it.
- **Border Light / Border / Border Strong**: hairlines divide rows and columns; the 2px
  ink rule (not a border token) opens sections.

### Status and its tints
Success / warning / error / info carry notices and double as the fixed categorical map
for resource types and entities (dots on outlined chips, `--type-*`). Info is a teal
(`oklch(52% 0.10 200)`) since 2.33, moved off the blue it used to share with the new
primary; it is also the AI-provenance and audiovisual hue. `--warning` sits at
`oklch(63.5% 0.18 60)` so the document and organisations dots clear 3:1 on all four light
surfaces (3.51 / 3.39 / 3.31 / 3.20). Each status has a paired ground: in light mode a
flat pastel at `oklch(96% …)` on the status hue; in dark mode a 22% `in oklab` mix of a
**darker, more saturated base than the foreground hue** — `oklch(50% 0.13 152)` success,
`oklch(55% 0.16 60)` warning, `oklch(50% 0.18 25)` error, `oklch(50% 0.10 200)` info —
because mixing the foreground hue into a lamplit ground produced a washed, hue-shifted plate.

### Encoding scales
- **Resource type / entity** (`--type-article` … `--type-entity-evenements`, 11 tokens): a
  fixed semantic map, rendered as dots. Article is the spot blue, publication the claret,
  audiovisual teal, document amber, reference muted, photograph green; of the five index
  types Personnes, Lieux and Organisations reuse info / success / warning, Sujets aliases
  `--series-5` (purple) and Événements `--series-9` (magenta). Declared in **both** theme
  blocks.
- **Categorical series** (`--series-1` … `--series-20`): the theme-owned chart palette.
  Slots 1 and 2 alias `--primary` and `--secondary` and flip with the theme; slots 3–20
  are literals, independent per theme, tuned in lightness only so every slot clears 3:1
  on all four surfaces of its theme. 2.33 re-picked the slots that crowded the new
  blue / claret / teal leads: 3, 9 (teal → magenta), 11 (steel → cornflower) and 20
  (teal → olive).

The dark palette is not a mechanical inversion: surfaces go warm (oklch hue ~70–80,
chroma ~0.012 — the "lamplit reading room"), primary is re-lit, and every composed token
that references a palette entry — the focus tokens, the selection wash, the panel set,
the overlay shadow — is **redeclared** in the dark block so it composes against dark
referents. Dark values live in `tokens.json` and the sidecar.

### Named Rules
**The One Plate Rule.** The press plate — a grayscale collage multiplied over a blue
ground built from the raw seed, through a 4px halftone dot screen — is the one big colour
statement. It prints twice: as the homepage hero, and at band height as the plate page
head of section pages. Everything else earns blue only as an accent. The ground is built
from `--primary-base`, never `--primary`, so the plate stays deep blue in dark mode.

**The Spot-Not-Paint Rule.** Blue marks what is live or current — links, focus, active
state, interpunct, dot, count, the one loud button, the plate. It is never heading text,
running prose, a badge fill, a tab fill, or a wash laid for atmosphere. Contrast is not
the limit (it clears 10:1); the second plate is. The seed is admin-configurable, so a
use that only works because of this particular blue is a use to avoid.

**The Dot-Not-Fill Rule.** Category is a dot on an outlined chip, never a filled or pastel
badge. Ten filled badges per screen is shouting; ten dots is an index.

**The Data-Only Secondary Rule.** The claret exists for data. If it appears on a button,
link, or focus ring, it is a bug.

**The Mirror-Both-Blocks Rule.** A custom property is substituted at computed-value time
on the element that *declares* it, so a token whose value contains `var(…)` must be
declared in **both** theme blocks, and the rule runs both directions. A light-only
composition is inherited frozen by every dark descendant; a dark-only declaration leaves a
manual light choice on a dark-OS machine unable to win back. The light and dark token
blocks are a **mixin pair** carrying the same names, and `npm run check:tokens` asserts it.

**The Three-to-One Mark Rule.** Every categorical mark — each `--series-*` slot and each
`--type-*` dot — clears 3:1 on all four surfaces of its theme. `npm run check:tokens`
measures it; a slot that fails moves in lightness, never in hue.

**The Published Scale Rule.** A colour that encodes data belongs in `tokens.json` with a
name, a light value and a dark value. Owning a scale means deciding it, never leaving it
unpublished in a consumer's source.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Besley (with Source Serif 4, Georgia fallback), loaded at 500 / 600 / 800 only
**Body Font:** Public Sans (system-ui fallback), 400–700
**Long-form Font:** Source Serif 4 (Georgia fallback), 400–600
**Label Font:** Fira Sans Condensed (Public Sans stack fallback), 500 / 600 only

**Character:** a Clarendon slab masthead over a rational sans, with a condensed press
gothic for the tracked capitals — the newspaper's voice and the instrument's legibility.
Besley, Source Serif 4 and Fira Sans Condensed carry every Arabic-transliteration glyph
`ḥ ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ ʿ ā ī ū` (Public Sans lacks `ʿ`). `--font-condensed` falls back to the body stack, so a failed load costs
width, never coverage. Body copy sets old-style proportional figures; headings set lining
figures.

### Hierarchy
The ramp is ten steps (0.6875 / 0.8125 / 0.9375 / 1.0625 / 1.1875 / 1.5 / 1.875rem, then
three clamps), fluid only at the top three. Headings step **up** one token at ≥768px, so
each display role names two sizes; the frontmatter records the ≥768px value.

- **Display** (800, `--text-5xl` clamp, 1.02): the homepage hero heading in white on the
  plate, at ≥1024px (`--text-3xl` / `--text-4xl` below). The masthead wordmark is the same
  voice smaller — Besley 800 at 1.1875rem, 1.5rem from 768px.
- **Headline** (600, `--text-4xl` clamp, 1.08): `h1`; the `--text-3xl` clamp below 768px.
  The plate page head's title is the masthead voice one step under the hero — Besley 800
  in white, 1.875rem / `--text-3xl` / `--text-4xl` at phone / 768 / 1024px, 1.02.
  A page title that is not on the plate is the `h1.title` section head: Besley 800 at
  1.875rem, opened by a 2px `--ink-strong` rule.
- **Subhead** (600, `--text-3xl` clamp, 1.25): `h2`; 1.875rem below 768px. Never painted
  in primary.
- **Title** (600, 1.875rem, 1.25): `h3` and ledger-row item titles; 1.5rem below 768px.
- **Section head** (800, 1.1875rem): the in-record section heads (How to cite, Linked
  resources, Archived web page) — an `h2` sized by the shared `section-head` mixin, ink,
  opened by the 2px ink rule. They name their section without shouting over it. A module
  block that renders its own section heading reproduces it from the tokens. Never primary.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): UI text in Public Sans; paragraphs run at 1.7.
- **Body-serif** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.7): article full text, ledes, long-form reading; long
  AI and OCR prose runs one step up at 1.1875rem.
- **Label** (600, 0.9375rem, sentence case, untracked): metadata `dt`s in `--ink`, form
  labels, disclosure toggles, hero stat captions, breadcrumbs (current crumb at 600).
- **Dateline** (Fira Sans Condensed 600, 0.8125rem, +0.08em, UPPERCASE): the
  `TYPE · PUBLISHER · DATE` line, carousel datelines and the news date headers. The nav
  strip and drawer share the condensed uppercase voice at 0.8125rem / 600 / +0.04em
  (drawer section links +0.08em, sub-entries 0.9375rem). In IwacVisualizations the same
  face sets chart axes, legends and tooltips. 0.6875rem (`--text-2xs`) is the absolute
  floor.

### Named Rules
**The Rationed Capitals Rule.** Tracked uppercase belongs to two places only, and is set in
`--font-condensed` in both: the nav strip (and the drawer that mirrors it) and datelines. Every other label — metadata `dt`s,
breadcrumbs, table headers, filter chips, hero stat captions, pagination, form labels,
buttons, disclosure toggles — is sentence case, untracked, weight 600 where it is a label.

**The Slab Tracking Rule.** Besley display sizes track at -0.01em (`--tracking-display`),
never the full -0.02em — slab serifs clog at tight tracking.

**The Lining Zero Rule.** Besley's `tnum` zero is broken (frozen at 55 units across
weights); use `font-feature-settings: "lnum"` for aligned numerals, never `tabular-nums`
with the display serif. All headings set `"kern" "liga" "dlig" "lnum"`.

**The No-14px Rule.** The type scale deliberately skips 14px; 11px (`--text-2xs`) is the
floor. Absolute `font-size` literals fail `npm run check:tokens`, inside `clamp()`,
`min()` and `max()` included — a fluid size composes `--text-*` steps. A weight the theme
does not load (Besley 700) fails it too.

**The Narrow-Space Figure Rule.** Thousands group with U+202F (narrow no-break space) in
every locale — pagination, hero figures and transcript word counts all pass through
`number_format($n, 0, '.', "\u{202F}")`. The decimal mark follows the page locale, never
the browser's (`12,5 %` fr, `12.5%` en), and date-only values format in UTC.

**The Bound Punctuation Rule.** French sets a space before `: ; ! ?` and `»` and after
`«`. Titles pass through the `FrenchSpacing` helper, which rewrites those spaces to
U+202F NARROW NO-BREAK SPACE — same gap, no line break before an orphaned colon.

## Layout

Content column capped at 1300px (`--content-max-width`); `--measure-narrow` (44rem),
`--measure-base` (52.5rem) and `--measure-wide` (72.5rem) remain for single elements, not
for prose blocks. Spacing runs a 4px-based numeric scale (`--space-1` … `--space-40`,
0.25–10rem) with semantic aliases (xs–3xl). The sticky masthead is 133px tall (160px at
≥1024px): wordmark row plus uppercase nav strip, closed by a 2px ink rule; below 1024px a
drawer with a slim ink hamburger takes over, and below 600px the wordmark swaps to the
per-site acronym (full title stays as the accessible name).

Breakpoints are a published contract (`tokens.json`): xs 400, sm 600, md 768, lg 1024,
xl 1200, xxl 1460px. `min-width` sits **on** a published breakpoint and `max-width` at
**breakpoint − 1px**. `@container` queries are exempt.

Page blocks are ruled sections on one flat `--surface`: the direct children of the page
grid carry no fill, border, radius, shadow or inline padding, and each section after the
first opens on the 2px `--ink-strong` rule (not the one directly under a page title, whose
`h1` has its own rule or is the plate). Blocks nested in a block group are one section.

A section page that opens on its page-title block (Explore, Browse, Index, References,
About and their sub-pages) opens on the **plate page head**: that block printed as a
full-bleed band in the hero's recipe, flush under the masthead rule, crumb line above a
white Besley 800 title, about 160px tall at 1440. It prints no image below 768px (flat
blue ground and dot screen only) and has no animation. `helper/PageHead.php` decides; it
never appears on resource pages, the homepage, or a page-title block further down a page.

Density rules the page grammar: result lists are flat ledger rows separated by
hairlines, never boxed cards; multi-value metadata flows inline with primary
interpuncts; resource metadata sits in a 168px-label two-column ledger with hairline row
separators, selected with `> dl > .property > dd` so a nested annotation `<dl>` never
inherits the label column. A long body — a full OCR transcript — is cut to an opening
extract with the remainder behind a native `<details>`, so the sections below stay
reachable.

### Named Rules
**The Fill-the-Width Rule.** Prose never sits in a narrow capped column with an empty half
beside it. Short prose (an HTML block under ~2,400 characters with no table, figure, embed
or numbered list; the AI summary on an item) flows into balanced newspaper columns from
1024px — 28rem column width, `--space-10` gap, a 1px `--border` column rule, headings
spanning all columns. Long prose stays one column at the full container width; phones
get one column. `helper/ProseColumns.php` decides.

**The One-Spelling Breakpoint Rule.** `min-width` sits on a published breakpoint and
`max-width` at breakpoint − 1px (`#{$md - 1px}`), never Bootstrap's − 0.02px — carrying
both idioms in files that reflow the same header is how the halves of a pair both match.
Guards in this repo and in both consumer modules assert it, including `matchMedia()`
strings and `media=""` attributes.

**The Separator-Ends-The-Line Rule.** An inline separator between repeated values is drawn
with `::after` on the preceding value, never `::before` on the following one, so a wrap
never opens a line on an interpunct that reads as a bullet.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, by conviction and now by token. The page carries no elevation: panels, cards,
buttons, rows, chips and filter bars cast no shadow, and hierarchy comes from rules and
typography. `--shadow-xs/sm/md`, `--panel-shadow` and the three `--glow-*` tokens are
published as a transparent zero shadow (`0 0 0 0 transparent`, not `none`, so composed
`box-shadow` lists stay valid) because both modules still consume the names. Exactly one
real elevation remains: `--shadow-lg`, a small separating shadow for what genuinely floats
over the page — the drawer, menus, the language switcher, tooltips, annotation popovers,
the typeahead. `backdrop-filter` glass exists in one place: the sticky header
(`--surface-overlay`); opaque popovers use `--surface`.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **shadow-lg** (`0 4px 12px -4px rgba(9, 11, 15, 0.18), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.06)`
  light; struck from pure black at higher alpha in dark): overlays only.
- **shadow-xs / sm / md, panel-shadow, glow-xs / sm / md** (`0 0 0 0 transparent`): flat,
  kept for consumers.
- **focus-outline** (`2px solid` `--focus-color`): the default focus indicator, offset 2px.
- **ring-focus / ring-focus-sm** (`0 0 0 3px` at 30% / `0 0 0 2px` at 22% of `--primary`):
  the box-shadow ring, for inputs (with a primary border) and for controls whose outline
  an overflow ancestor would clip.

### Named Rules
**The Square-and-Flat Rule.** `--radius-sm/md/lg` are 0 and the page casts no shadow. A
new component that needs a corner or a lift to read as a unit is asking for a rule instead.

**The Overlay-Only Shadow Rule.** `--shadow-lg` is for things that float over the page and
dismiss — drawer, menu, popover, tooltip, typeahead. A panel in the flow never takes it.

**The Glass Once Rule.** Backdrop-filter belongs to the sticky header and nowhere else.

**The Rules-Not-Boxes Rule.** Before adding a card, try a 2px ink rule or a hairline. Boxes
are a last resort for true panels (charts). The AI lede, the linked-resources filter bar,
the transcript disclosure and every page block were boxes once and are rules now.

**The Outline-First Focus Rule.** `--focus-outline` at 2px offset is the focus indicator.
The `--ring-focus` halo is the exception for inputs and clipped controls — paired with
`outline: 2px solid transparent`, never `outline: none`, so forced-colors mode repaints a
real outline. The one legal `outline: none` is `:focus:not(:focus-visible)`. On the plate,
focus rings are white.

## Shapes

Rectilinear and editorial. Every control, chip, tab, input, panel, figure and thumbnail is
square-cornered (`--radius-sm/md/lg` = 0). `--radius-full` belongs to dots, status pips,
switch thumbs and the circular add/remove-value and masthead icon controls — never a
control's outline, never a tab or toggle. No blobs, no organic masks, no skewed sections.
The recurring silhouette is the horizontal rule: 2px ink to open (`h1.title`, page
sections, section heads, footer top, almanac figures, the AI lede) and to close the
header; hairlines to divide rows, newspaper columns and footer bands, and to carry
"continued" labels. Accent lines come in 2px (`--accent-line-sm`) and 3px
(`--accent-line-md`). Coloured side-stripes (`border-left` / `border-inline-start` in a
brand, status or categorical colour) are banned and Stylelint refuses them; the one
side rule that stays is the blockquote's `--blockquote-border`, which is typography.

## Components

### Buttons
The register's signature inversion: **the default is quiet; the loud one opts in.**
- **Base `<button>`:** transparent, 1px `--border` outline, ink text, square, padding
  0.5rem 1.5rem, 0.9375rem at weight 500; hover fills `--surface-sunken` and strengthens
  the border to `--border-strong`. No shadow, no lift.
- **Primary (`.btn--primary` or any submit control):** a flat square of the spot colour —
  `--primary` fill, transparent border, `--ink-on-primary` text; hover `--primary-hover`,
  pressed `--primary-active`. No halo, no lift since 2.33. Disabled falls to `--muted` at 70%.
- **Secondary (`.btn--secondary`):** outlined in primary, transparent fill; hover washes
  with `--selection-bg` and deepens border and text to `--primary-hover`.
- **Icon buttons:** borderless primary glyphs sized off the control scale (1.75–3rem); a
  `::after` overlay extends hit areas toward 44px.
- **Focus (all):** `--focus-outline`, offset 2px.

### Controls grammar
Two kinds of switch, two treatments, and the loud fill is neither.
- **Page-level tab sets:** ruled tabs — ink text on the page ground, no box; active is
  `--ink-strong` text on a 2px `--primary` underline.
- **In-panel toggles** (list ↔ grid, chart mode, filter chips): outlined square chips;
  active is a `--primary` border plus a 10% `in oklab` primary wash, label or glyph in
  `--primary-hover`. The browse grid/list toggle is the reference build: a `role="group"`
  pair of icon buttons whose state is `aria-pressed`, both left enabled.

**The Two-Switch Rule.** Page-level tab sets are ruled tabs; in-panel toggles are outlined
square chips with a primary border and wash when active. Neither is ever a filled-primary
active state — the brand fill belongs to the one loud action — and `--radius-full` is for
dots, thumbs and circular icon controls, never a tab or toggle.

### Icons
Every UI glyph is a Lucide SVG painted through `icon-mask()` in `currentColor`; no icon
font loads. The mixin restates the glyph in a system colour under `forced-colors: active`
(CanvasText / ButtonText / LinkText by context), because a bare mask paints nothing there.

**The Mask-Not-Font Icon Rule.** A new glyph is a Lucide SVG in `asset/img/` behind
`@include icon-mask(url, $forced)`, with `ButtonText` inside buttons and `LinkText` inside
links. A rule that sets `background-color` after the mixin re-hides the glyph; set `color`.

### Chips / Tags
Outlined square chips: transparent fill, 1px `--border-strong`, 0.8125rem at weight 600,
sentence case, `--ink-light` text, padding 2px 0.75rem; hover inks text and border to
`--ink-strong`. Resource-type and entity chips add a categorical dot from the fixed
`--type-*` map. Active/selected = primary border + `--selection-bg` wash, never a fill.

### Inputs / Fields
Full-width on `--panel-bg` (`--surface`), `--panel-border` (1px `--border`), square,
padding 0.5rem 1rem, 1.0625rem, line-height 1.4. Focus swaps the border to primary and
adds the 3px `--ring-focus` over a transparent outline — no transform. Placeholder in
`--muted`. Checkboxes are custom-drawn, square, 2px border, filled primary with an
`--ink-on-primary` tick when checked.

### Navigation
Masthead: Besley 800 wordmark row, then the uppercase Fira Sans Condensed strip
(0.8125rem, 600, +0.04em); the active section gets a 2px primary tab sitting on the closing ink rule. The
mobile drawer speaks the same register — solid `--surface`, uppercase entries on hairline
rules, `--shadow-lg` because it floats — no glass, no pill buttons.

### Plate Page Head (signature)
A section page's title printed on the press plate: full-bleed band, ground
`color-mix(in oklab, seed 88%, white 12%)`, a top-to-foot black scrim (6% → 22% → 42%),
the 4px halftone dot screen, and from 768px the grayscale collage multiplied in. White
Besley 800 title (`max-width: 22em`, balanced wrap) at the foot; the breadcrumb above it in
92% white with white underlines, a white current-page dot and white focus rings. Dark mode
banks the ground down to the seed mixed 24% toward black — still the seed, never the
re-lit `--primary`. Forced colours drop the layers for `Canvas` with a 2px `CanvasText`
rule under the head; print sets it as ink on the section-head rule.

### Dateline (signature)
`TYPE · PUBLISHER · DATE` in tracked uppercase Fira Sans Condensed, `--ink-light`, with bold
primary interpuncts — the recurring identity device on items, ledger rows and carousel
captions. Its right edge is the utility slot: a quiet **Cite** jump (quote mark, hairline
underline) that links to the citation panel's stable `#how-to-cite` anchor and is revealed
only where that panel is actually in the admin-configured stack.

### Ledger Row (signature)
The result-list unit: hairline-ruled `<li>`, dateline eyebrow, Besley title, 2-line
snippet, quiet interpunct source line, outlined square type chip with categorical dot. No
boxes, no shadows, no hover lift. Linked resources on a record speak the same grammar —
opened by a section head on the ink rule, filtered by a hairline-underlined control bar.

### KPI / Almanac Figure (signature)
2px rule on top, Besley 800 numeral with `"lnum"`, a sentence-case 600 caption; the
featured figure's rule turns primary (white on the plate). No card boxes, no gradients, no
hover lift. On the hero the figures sit in two ruled tiers — the four live counts, then a
quieter tier whose column count follows how many figures the snapshot supplied
(`banner__substats--n<count>`).

### Homepage Hero
The press plate at full height: a labelled `<section>` named by its `h1`, the collage with
a one-time Ken Burns settle under a static dot screen, white Besley 800 heading, the
search, then the almanac tiers.

### Disclosure / "Continued" Rule (signature)
Progressive disclosure for over-long bodies, on a native `<details>`: the summary is a
press "continued" line — a 1px `--border` hairline carrying a sentence-case 600 label in
`--ink-light` and a chevron mask that rotates on open; hover turns label and rule primary.
Never a button box; the UA marker is suppressed and the label swaps by `display: none` so
only one wording reaches the accessible name.

### Pagination & Breadcrumbs
Pagination sits between hairline top/bottom borders, quiet controls, no primary tint at
rest. Breadcrumbs are a plain sentence-case crumb line at 0.9375rem: `--muted` links,
primary on hover, `--ink-subtle` slashes, the current page in `--ink` at 600 behind a
primary dot. Link crumbs wrap, never truncate; only the current crumb ellipses, so the
trail stays one line and never pushes the document sideways on a phone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open sections with 2px ink rules and divide rows and columns with hairlines —
  typography is the architecture, rules are the joinery.
- **Do** keep every corner square and every in-flow surface shadowless; reserve
  `--shadow-lg` for overlays.
- **Do** flow short prose into balanced columns at wide viewports and let long prose take
  the full container width.
- **Do** declare every composed or aliased token in **both** theme blocks.
- **Do** publish any colour that encodes data as a named light/dark pair in `tokens.json`,
  and let the guards compare it.
- **Do** time motion from `--duration-fast/base/slow` (150 / 200 / 300ms), alone or as
  `--transition-*` on `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`.
- **Do** mark AI-generated fields with the EU "AI GENERATED" provenance treatment on the
  label, and open the lede on an ink rule.
- **Do** mix colours `in oklab`, derive primary variants from the seed, build the plate
  from `--primary-base`, and run `npm run check:tokens` before trusting any token name.
- **Do** test any new font against `ḥ ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ ʿ ā ī ū` before adopting it.
- **Do** give every interactive element a visible focus state — `--focus-outline` first —
  and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** round a corner or lift a panel, card or button with a shadow or glow.
- **Don't** set tracked uppercase anywhere but the nav strip and datelines.
- **Don't** put prose in a capped measure with an empty half beside it.
- **Don't** use warm cream or parchment surfaces — wrong register ("manuscript museum
  brochure").
- **Don't** add atmospheric gradients, gradient bars, glassmorphism (outside the sticky
  header), or coloured side-stripes.
- **Don't** give a tab or toggle a filled-primary active state, or a pill shape.
- **Don't** fill category badges, colour headings, or paint washes in the spot blue —
  category is a dot, blue is a second plate.
- **Don't** use cold blue-gray dark surfaces — dark mode is warm ("lamplit"), hue ~70–80.
- **Don't** box result rows, filter bars, linked-resource lists, page blocks or the AI lede
  into cards.
- **Don't** replace an outline focus indicator with the `--ring-focus` halo for convenience,
  and never with `outline: none` alone.
- **Don't** use bouncing/elastic easing or hover-triggered hero animations (the one-time
  Ken Burns settle is the sanctioned exception; the plate page head has no animation).
- **Don't** pair Besley with `tabular-nums` (broken tnum zero) or track it tighter than
  -0.01em.
- **Don't** write an absolute `font-size` literal, inside `clamp()` included, or spell the
  "below" half of a breakpoint pair any way but breakpoint − 1px.
