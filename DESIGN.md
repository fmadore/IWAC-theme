# Design System: IWAC Theme

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Research Broadsheet"**

A 20th-century broadsheet operated as a scientific instrument. The site is a scholarly
database — LLM sentiment models, LDA topics, embeddings, IIIF — that carries the visual
grammar of the newspapers it preserves: masthead wordmark, uppercase section strip,
thick 2px ink rules, datelines, ledger-style result rows, a duotone press-collage hero.
The execution is a modern broadsheet's digital archive run by a university press:
newspaper typographic conventions with Linear/Stripe-grade product polish.

Density is a feature, not a compromise: researchers want more items per screen, flat
ledger rows over boxed cards, metadata flowing inline with interpunct separators.
Restraint is the resting state — the quiet control is the default and loudness opts in.
Confirmed anti-references: not a small museum's website, not a warm editorial magazine,
not a generic dashboard; no cream or parchment, no pastiche of aged paper.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Newspaper grammar (rules, datelines, ledgers) executed with product-grade polish
- Density over comfort; typography as architecture, rules as joinery
- Near-white cool-neutral light mode; warm "lamplit reading room" dark mode
- One brand accent used sparingly; category encoded as dots, never fills
- AI-generated content visibly marked (computational honesty)

## Colors

An OKLCH-derived palette: near-white warm-tinted surfaces, cool-neutral inks, and one
burnt-orange accent, all cascading from an admin-overridable seed via
`color-mix(in oklab, …)`.

### Primary
- **IWAC Burnt Orange** (#ce4115): the sole UI accent, derived from seed `#e64a19`
  darkened 8% in light mode. Appears as focus states, current/active states (nav tab,
  active filter chips), dateline interpuncts, categorical dots, key counts, and the
  duotone hero plate — never as heading text, per-row badge fills, or decorative wash.
  Hover deepens to #b03710, active to #942c0c.

### Secondary
- **Slate** (#394f68): **data-only** — chart series 2 and corpus comparison in
  IwacVisualizations. Never buttons, links, focus, or any UI chrome.

### Neutral
- **Ink Strong** (#05070c): masthead rules, strongest emphasis.
- **Ink** (#13161c): body text.
- **Ink Light / Ink Subtle / Muted** (#3f4349 / #5a5e63 / #66696e): secondary text,
  uppercase metadata labels, quiet chrome.
- **Surface / Raised / Sunken / Background** (#fdfcfb / #faf8f6 / #f4f1ef / #f7f5f3):
  near-white at chroma ~0.002 — an imperceptible warm tint, never cream.
- **Border Light / Border / Border Strong** (#e2e5e8 / #ced1d6 / #aeb1b7): hairlines
  divide rows; the 2px ink rule (not a border token) opens sections.

Status colors (#2e9052 success, #de7000 warning, #c9222b error, #037ac0 info) double as
the fixed categorical map for resource types and entities (dots on outlined chips).

The dark palette is not a mechanical inversion: surfaces go warm
(oklch hue ~70–80, chroma ~0.012 — the "lamplit reading room"), and primary lightens
to #ec653f. Dark values live in `tokens.json` and the sidecar.

### Named Rules
**The One Plate Rule.** The duotone hero — a grayscale collage multiplied over a primary
ground — is the one big color statement on the site. Everything else earns primary only
as an accent.

**The Dot-Not-Fill Rule.** Category is a dot on an outlined chip, never a filled or
pastel badge. Ten filled badges per screen is shouting; ten dots is an index.

**The Data-Only Secondary Rule.** #394f68 exists for charts. If it appears on a button,
link, or focus ring, it is a bug.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Besley (with Source Serif 4, Georgia fallback)
**Body Font:** Public Sans (system-ui fallback)
**Long-form Font:** Source Serif 4 (Georgia fallback)

**Character:** a Clarendon slab masthead over a rational sans — the newspaper's voice
and the instrument's legibility. All three faces are verified against the
Arabic-transliteration diacritics `ḥ ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ ʿ ā ī ū`.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp 3.25–4.75rem): masthead wordmark and homepage hero only.
- **Headline** (600, clamp 2.25–3rem, 1.08): page titles (`h1`), opened by a 2px ink
  rule; steps up one size at ≥768px.
- **Title** (600, 1.5rem): section heads (`h3`) and item titles in ledger rows.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.5): UI prose in Public Sans.
- **Body-serif** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.7): article full text, ledes, long-form reading, at
  measures 44–52.5rem.
- **Label** (600, 0.8125rem, +0.04em, UPPERCASE): datelines, metadata labels, chips,
  the nav strip; drops to 0.6875rem (`--text-2xs`, the absolute floor) for the densest
  eyebrows. Wider tracking (+0.08em) exists for the most spaced settings.

### Named Rules
**The Slab Tracking Rule.** Besley display sizes track at -0.01em, never the full
-0.02em — slab serifs clog at tight tracking.

**The Lining Zero Rule.** Besley's `tnum` zero is broken (frozen at 55 units across
weights); use `font-feature-settings: "lnum"` for aligned numerals, never
`tabular-nums` with the display serif.

**The No-14px Rule.** The type scale deliberately skips 14px; 11px (`--text-2xs`) is
the floor. Absolute `font-size` literals fail `npm run check:tokens` — use `--text-*`
tokens.

## Layout

Content column capped at 1300px (`--content-max-width`); reading measures at 44rem
(narrow, article text), 52.5rem (base), and 72.5rem (wide). Spacing runs a 4px-based
scale (0.25–10rem) with semantic aliases (xs–3xl). The sidebar, where used, holds 27%.
The sticky masthead is 133px tall (160px at ≥1024px): wordmark row plus uppercase nav
strip, closed by a 2px ink rule; below 1024px a drawer with a slim ink hamburger takes
over, and below 600px the wordmark swaps to the per-site acronym (full title stays as
the accessible name).

Breakpoints are a published contract (`tokens.json`): xs 400, sm 600, md 768, lg 1024,
xl 1200, xxl 1460px. Guards in both consumer modules enforce `min-width` on a published
breakpoint and `max-width` at breakpoint − 1.

Density rules the page grammar: result lists are flat ledger rows separated by
hairlines, never boxed cards; multi-value metadata flows inline with primary
interpuncts; resource metadata sits in a 168px-label two-column ledger with hairline
row separators.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by conviction, layered by exception. Hierarchy comes from rules and typography;
shadows are reserved for true panels (charts, the AI lede, dropdowns) and the opt-in
primary button glow. Rows, tags, and chips never cast shadows — they are drawn with
hairlines. `backdrop-filter` glass exists in exactly one place: the sticky header
(`--surface-overlay`, rgba(253,252,251,0.94)); opaque popovers use `--surface`.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **shadow-xs → shadow-lg** (`0 1px 2px 0 rgba(20,22,27,0.06)` up to
  `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(9,11,15,0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(20,22,27,0.06)`): neutral,
  panel-only ambient depth.
- **panel-shadow** (= shadow-sm): the default panel treatment.
- **glow-xs / glow-sm / glow-md** (primary-tinted, e.g. `0 2px 6px -1px
  rgba(206,65,21,0.25)`): reserved for the filled-primary control's rest / hover
  states.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Once Rule.** Backdrop-filter belongs to the sticky header and nowhere else.

**The Rules-Not-Boxes Rule.** Before adding a shadowed card, try a 2px ink rule or a
hairline. Boxes are a last resort for true panels.

## Shapes

Rectilinear and editorial. Text controls and panels take modest radii (0.375 / 0.5 /
0.75rem); pills (`9999px`) are reserved for uppercase tag chips and circular icon
controls. No blobs, no organic masks, no skewed sections. The recurring silhouette is
the horizontal rule: 2px ink to open (`h1`, footer top, KPI figures) and to close the
header, hairlines to divide. Accent lines come in 2px (`--accent-line-sm`) and 3px
(`--accent-line-md`). Colored side-stripes (`border-left` accents) are banned — use
dots, full-width rules, or nothing.

## Components

### Buttons
The register's signature inversion: **the default is quiet; the loud one opts in.**
- **Base `<button>`:** transparent, 1px `--border` outline, ink text, radius 0.5rem,
  padding 0.5rem 1.5rem, 0.9375rem at weight 500; hover fills `--surface-sunken` and
  strengthens the border. No shadow, no lift.
- **Primary (`.btn--primary` or any submit control):** primary fill, white text,
  `--glow-sm` halo; hover lifts -1px with `--glow-md`; active returns flat on
  `--primary-active` with `--glow-xs`.
- **Secondary (`.btn--secondary`):** outlined in primary, transparent fill; hover
  washes with `--selection-bg` (rgba(206,65,21,0.14)).
- **Icon buttons:** borderless primary glyphs sized off the control scale
  (1.75–3rem); a `::after` overlay extends hit areas to 44px.
- **Focus (all):** 2px primary outline, offset 2px.

### Chips / Tags
Uppercase outlined pills: transparent fill, 1px `--border-strong` outline, full radius,
0.8125rem at weight 600, +0.04em tracking, `--ink-light` text. Resource-type and entity
chips add a categorical dot from the fixed semantic map. Active/selected = primary
border + `--selection-bg` wash, never a filled orange.

### Inputs / Fields
Full-width on `--surface` (panel bg), 1px `--border`, radius 0.5rem, padding
0.5rem 1rem, 1.0625rem. Focus swaps the border to primary and adds the 3px
`--ring-focus` (rgba(206,65,21,0.3)) — no transform. Placeholder in `--muted`.
Checkboxes/radios are custom-drawn with 2px borders.

### Navigation
Masthead: Besley 800 wordmark row, then an uppercase Public Sans strip; the active
section gets a 2px primary tab sitting on the closing ink rule. The mobile drawer
speaks the same register — solid `--surface`, uppercase entries on hairline rules — no
glass, no pill buttons.

### Dateline (signature)
`TYPE · PUBLISHER · DATE` in tracked uppercase label type with primary interpuncts —
the recurring identity device on items, ledger rows, and cards.

### Ledger Row (signature)
The result-list unit: hairline-ruled `<li>`, dateline eyebrow, Besley title, 2-line
snippet, quiet interpunct source line, outlined type chip with categorical dot. No
boxes, no shadows, no hover lift.

### KPI / Almanac Figure (signature)
2px ink rule on top, uppercase eyebrow, Besley numeral with `"lnum"`; the featured
figure's rule turns primary. No card boxes, no gradients.

### Pagination & Breadcrumbs
Pagination sits between hairline top/bottom borders, quiet controls, no primary tint at
rest. Breadcrumbs are a plain uppercase crumb line, current page marked with a primary
dot.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open sections with 2px ink rules and divide rows with hairlines — typography
  is the architecture, rules are the joinery.
- **Do** keep transitions 150–300ms on `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`; real objects
  decelerate smoothly.
- **Do** mark AI-generated fields with the EU "AI GENERATED" provenance treatment.
- **Do** mix colors `in oklab`, derive primary variants from the seed, and run
  `npm run check:tokens` before trusting any token name.
- **Do** test any new font against `ḥ ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ ʿ ā ī ū` before adopting it.
- **Do** give every interactive element a visible focus state and respect
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use warm cream or parchment surfaces — wrong register ("manuscript museum
  brochure").
- **Don't** add atmospheric gradients, gradient bars, glassmorphism (outside the sticky
  header), or colored side-stripes.
- **Don't** fill category badges or color `h2`s in primary — category is a dot, primary
  is an accent.
- **Don't** use cold blue-gray dark surfaces — dark mode is warm ("lamplit"), hue
  ~70–80.
- **Don't** box result rows into cards or stack decorative effects on one component.
- **Don't** use bouncing/elastic easing or hover-triggered hero animations (the
  one-time Ken Burns settle is the sanctioned exception).
- **Don't** pair Besley with `tabular-nums` (broken tnum zero) or track it tighter than
  -0.01em.
